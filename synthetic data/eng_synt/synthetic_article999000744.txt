Who knew that binge-watching cat videos could lead to breakthroughs in quantum physics?”  The researchers conducted a series of tests involving a group of dedicated procrastinators, who were asked to complete various tasks. Half of the group was given strict deadlines, while the other half was told to simply “figure it out later.” Astonishingly, the procrastinators not only completed their tasks, but they did so with such flair and creativity that the deadlines became mere footnotes in their illustrious careers.  One notable case involved a student who crafted an award-winning short story due the day after his final. He reportedly had the piece finished just moments before submitting it, all while claiming he was “just letting the ideas marinate.” His secret? A steady diet of cereal and panic-induced adrenaline.

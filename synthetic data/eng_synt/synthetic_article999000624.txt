In a groundbreaking initiative to address the pressing issue of global warming, the International Association of Extreme Couch Potatoes (IAECP) has officially launched the first “Couch Olympics” to promote sedentary lifestyles as a legitimate sport. Billed as the “most thrilling event” for those who believe that exertion is for the overly ambitious, the Couch Olympics promises to be both entertaining and utterly uninspiring.  The event will feature classic couch potato activities, such as synchronized remote control surfing, competitive snack consumption, and the ever-challenging “Napathon,” where participants will attempt to nap through various distractions, including loud house parties and a constant stream of cat videos. “We’re here to redefine athleticism,” said Couch Olympics director Barry Slothman, who assured reporters that “the only sweating you’ll be doing is from laughing too hard at the TV screen.”  Enthusiasts are encouraged to participate in teams, with event organizers suggesting team names like “The Chill Pillars” and “The Layabouts.” Each team will be equipped with state-of-the-art reclining chairs, snack dispensers, and, of course, a personal supply of soft drinks to ensure that energy levels remain at an all-time low. “We believe that if everyone just sits down and shuts up for a minute, the world might just be a little less chaotic,” Slothman added.  While many are skeptical about the legitimacy of couch potato activities, the IAECP believes that the event will attract a massive following. “We’ve already seen a surge in registrations from individuals who were previously too lethargic to participate in any real sports,” said Slothman. “This is a chance for the underappreciated couch-sitters of the world to shine.”  Critics, however, are concerned that promoting such a lifestyle could have dire consequences. “What’s next?” asked local fitness guru, Jane Sweatpants. “Are we going to start giving out gold medals for binge-watching entire seasons of TV shows in one sitting? Oh wait, we already are!”   Despite the backlash, the Couch Olympics has garnered endorsements from local junk food chains, with fast-food giant BingeBurger pledging to sponsor the event with free burgers for all competitors. “We understand that competition can be intense,” said BingeBurger spokesperson, Chuck Gulp, “but we believe that the real battle lies in who can eat the most while doing absolutely nothing.”  As the date of the Couch Olympics approaches, fans eagerly anticipate a festival of inactivity that could change the face of sports forever—one potato chip at a time.

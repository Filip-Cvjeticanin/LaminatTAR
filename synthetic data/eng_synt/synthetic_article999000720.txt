This explains why they can vanish the moment a vet’s appointment is mentioned.”  According to the study, 87% of cat owners have reported instances of their pets performing seemingly impossible feats of agility, such as jumping from a standing position to the top of a refrigerator or squeezing themselves into a box half their size. These remarkable skills have now been attributed to their hidden ninja training, which they practice rigorously while you’re at work.  Interviews with several cats have shed light on their covert operations. A local tabby, Mr. Mittens, revealed, “When my human thinks I’m napping, I’m actually perfecting my stealth moves. I can even sneak up on the vacuum cleaner without making a sound. The ultimate test of my ninja abilities.”  Furthermore, the research found that cats possess an impressive repertoire of combat techniques, including “The Pounce of Doom” and “The Sneaky Paw Swipe.” These moves are often demonstrated on unsuspecting human feet at random intervals. “It’s all part of the training,” explained Mr. Mittens. “Humans need to learn to stay alert at all times.”  As if that wasn't enough, the study also uncovered a shocking statistic: 64% of cats reported that they have held their humans hostage by refusing to get off their laps during critical moments of TV shows. Experts believe this is a strategic maneuver to gain ultimate control over the remote.  In light of these findings, the Institute of Feline Studies is now advocating for a national “Cat Ninja Awareness Month,” during which humans will be encouraged to respect their cats' secretive lifestyles and provide them with the necessary training gear, including tiny black belts and miniature throwing stars (which are, of course, just shiny cat toys).   So, the next time your cat stares at you with those piercing eyes, remember: it’s not just plotting your demise; it’s training for its next big ninja mission.

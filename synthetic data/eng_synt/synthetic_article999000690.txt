In an unprecedented move that has sent shockwaves through the culinary world, the International Association of Culinary Experts (IACE) has officially declared that pineapple will henceforth be recognized as the world’s most versatile topping. This announcement comes on the heels of an extensive study aimed at resolving an age-old debate: whether pineapple belongs on pizza or if it should remain confined to tropical drinks and sad fruit salads.  The IACE’s decision was met with both jubilance and disbelief. “Finally, we can put the pineapple controversy to rest,” said Dr. Basil Herbington, a leading food scientist and self-proclaimed pizza enthusiast. “Our rigorous research, which involved extensive taste tests and a few too many late-night slices, has proven that pineapple is not only acceptable on pizza but also improves virtually every dish known to mankind. We even tried it on chocolate cake, and let me tell you, it was a revelation.”  Among the unusual applications for pineapple touted by the IACE is its newfound role in haute cuisine, where chefs are now encouraged to incorporate the fruit into traditional dishes. Spaghetti with pineapple is expected to replace the classic marinara in trendy restaurants, and the beloved French dish Coq au Vin is set to be reimagined as Coq au Pineapple, much to the chagrin of traditionalists everywhere.  Social media erupted with reactions to the announcement, revealing a fervent divide between pineapple lovers and those still clinging to the notion that fruit should be relegated to breakfast items. “I can’t believe they’re doing this,” tweeted one outraged user. “Next, they’ll be telling us that ketchup is a smoothie.” Meanwhile, proponents of pineapple pizza hosted a celebratory live stream, where they created a 12-hour-long pizza-making marathon featuring endless combinations of toppings—yes, pineapple included.  The IACE has also suggested that pineapple could be used in new and exciting ways. Suggested uses include pineapple-based toothpaste, which promises to freshen breath while keeping your pizza-loving conscience clear. They even proposed a pineapple-scented candle as a must-have home accessory, perfect for setting the mood for an evening of, you guessed it, pizza night.  As the world grapples with the ramifications of this bold declaration, experts are left to ponder the next big food controversy on the horizon. Will it be the great avocado toast debate, or perhaps a reexamination of whether chocolate belongs in chili? Until then, pizza lovers unite—grab your pineapple, and remember, you are now on the right side of culinary history.

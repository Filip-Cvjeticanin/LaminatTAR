However, G.O.O.E.I. remains unfazed. “Listen, if we can get people to pay for doing absolutely nothing, who are we to judge?” Slothman quipped, as she practiced her deep breathing techniques on a couch that she claims to have “bonded” with over the years. Fitness brands are now pushing “couch yoga” classes, where participants can stretch from the comfort of their sofa, while motivational speakers have begun offering courses on “how to avoid doing things effectively.” The self-help sector is booming, with titles like “The Joy of Not Trying” and “How to Win at Napping: Strategies for Success” flying off the virtual shelves.  As the world prepares for this revolutionary shift, one thing is certain: the next generation will likely look back on 2023 as the year when sloths officially became the role models of productivity.
